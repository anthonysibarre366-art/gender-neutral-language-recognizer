Annual Report of the Student Council

The chairman opened the meeting and thanked every volunteer. Ladies and gentlemen, tonight we look back at a year of progress for all of mankind's smallest community: our campus.

The policeman on duty and the fireman from the nearby station joined the safety drill. The Chairman later reminded everyone that the drill is for the benefit of the whole school. A stewardess from the partner airline spoke about emergency procedures, and the headmaster closed the program.

Each team lead should tell the committee what he or she needs for next term. The council will rely on volunteer manpower, so please sign up early. A craftsman will build the stage, and the salesman from the local shop offered a discount on man-made decorations.

The CHAIRMAN will announce the new officers on Friday.
